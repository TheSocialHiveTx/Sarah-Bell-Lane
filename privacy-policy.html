Privacy Policy

This policy describes how information is collected and used on this website.

NOTICE: This privacy policy is provided as a starting template only. It requires review and approval by a licensed attorney before publication. It does not constitute legal advice. [DATE LAST REVIEWED: PLACEHOLDER]

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Sarah Bell Lane Home Loans collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users of this website. This policy applies to the website and all products and services offered by Sarah Bell Lane through Preferred Funding Inc.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at [EMAIL ADDRESS].

2. Information We Collect

Contact Form Submissions

We collect personal identification information from Users when they voluntarily submit information through our contact forms. This may include name, email address, phone number, and details about your mortgage goals. We strongly advise users not to submit sensitive financial information, such as Social Security numbers or bank account details, through our contact forms.

Technical Information

We may collect non-personal identification information about Users whenever they interact with our Site. Non-personal identification information may include the browser name, the type of computer or device, and technical information about Users' means of connection to our Site, such as the operating system, the Internet service providers utilized, IP addresses, and other similar information.

Cookies

Our Site may use "cookies" to enhance User experience and for analytics purposes. User's web browser places cookies on their hard drive for record-keeping purposes and sometimes to track information about them. You may choose to set your web browser to refuse cookies, or to alert you when cookies are being sent. If you do so, note that some parts of the Site may not function properly.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, provide customer service, improve our website, and send periodic emails related to your mortgage inquiries. We do not sell, trade, or rent Users' personal identification information to others.

4. The Preferred Funding Application Portal

When you choose to apply for a mortgage by clicking "Apply Now," you will leave this website and enter the secure application portal operated by Preferred Funding Inc. The Preferred Funding system maintains its own separate privacy practices, terms of use, and data security measures. We strongly recommend reviewing the privacy policy of Preferred Funding Inc. once you enter their portal.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party service providers to help us operate our business and the Site or administer activities on our behalf, such as Google Analytics for website traffic analysis or form processing services. These third parties have access to your personal information only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose. Each of these services maintains their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We adopt appropriate data collection, storage and processing practices and security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction of your personal information and data stored on our Site. However, no internet transmission is ever fully secure or error-free. Please do not send sensitive personal or financial data via unencrypted email or contact forms.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as is reasonably necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. You may contact us to request the removal of your information from our systems, subject to legal and regulatory retention requirements.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, update, or delete your personal information. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at [EMAIL ADDRESS].

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy periodically. When we do, we will revise the updated date at the top of this page. We encourage Users to frequently check this page for any changes to stay informed about how we are helping to protect the personal information we collect.
