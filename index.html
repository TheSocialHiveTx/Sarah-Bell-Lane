Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the mortgage lending process.

How do I start the mortgage process?
The best way to start is by completing a secure online application through the Preferred Funding, LLC secure portal. Alternatively, you can contact Sarah directly to discuss your goals before applying.
What is the difference between prequalification and preapproval?
Prequalification is an initial estimate of how much you might be able to borrow based on unverified information. Preapproval involves a thorough review of your verified documentation and credit history, providing a stronger indication of your borrowing capacity.
How much down payment do I need?
Down payment requirements vary significantly by program. While some loans require 20%, others may allow 3.5%, 3%, or even zero down for eligible borrowers and properties.
What credit score is needed to qualify?
Credit score requirements depend on the specific loan program. FHA and VA loans often offer more flexible credit guidelines, while Conventional and Jumbo loans typically require higher scores.
