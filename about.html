Meet Your Mortgage Professional

Experience, Communication, and Personal Mortgage Guidance

With 30 years of mortgage lending experience, Sarah Bell Lane provides personal guidance, clear communication, and dedicated support from application through closing.

A Lending Professional Who Has Seen Every Market

Over the past 30 years, the mortgage industry has seen significant changes, but the core of what makes a successful lending experience remains the same: knowledgeable guidance, personal attention, and reliable execution. I bring three decades of practical expertise to help you successfully navigate your home financing journey.

Working in conjunction with Preferred Funding, LLC, I am able to offer my clients a wide array of competitive financing solutions backed by the strength of a dedicated lending organization. Whether you are purchasing your first home or refinancing your current one, you can count on me to be an active partner in your transaction.

I believe in direct communication. When you work with me, you will have a personal point of contact rather than being passed between automated systems or different departments. From your initial consultation through the closing of your loan, I remain dedicated to your success.

Approach

The Lending Process Should Be Understandable

For many people, obtaining a mortgage is the largest financial transaction they will make. The terminology, paperwork, and requirements can sometimes feel overwhelming. It is my job to demystify the process.

I take the time to explain the details, outline the timeline, and set clear expectations. When borrowers know what to expect, the entire experience becomes significantly less stressful.

By focusing on education and open communication, I aim to empower my clients to make informed, confident decisions regarding their home financing.
